Procedural audio

The four cues are original deterministic synthesis created for this repository. They use no external recordings, samples, music or speech. Recipe version 2 generates mono buffers at runtime.

  • Room air: seeded noise through a low-pass filter; a two-second loop.
  • Door motion: filtered noise with 16 Hz amplitude modulation and a quiet 110 Hz tone; a one-second loop.
  • Furniture roll: filtered noise with 16 Hz amplitude modulation and a quiet 75 Hz tone; a one-second loop.
  • Screen switch: a decaying rising chirp starting at 660 Hz; a 0.12-second cue.
